Upload the imprest cash book

Upload the workbook as the office keeps it. The importer reads the year sheet, the EXPENSE column, and the DETAILS head. It never creates a head.

How the imprest sheet should look

A B C D E
Date Description INCOME EXPENSE DETAILS
2026-01-20 TNT to Pay tier 2 and documentation 35.00 T&T
Bags of satchet water @6*10 60.00 REFRESHMENT

The date is written once and carries down until the next date. Only the EXPENSE amount and the DETAILS head are saved. The DETAILS value must match one of the existing imprest heads exactly; uploading never creates one. Rows with no date to carry, no amount, or no description are reported and skipped. The imprest book can repeat the same day's payments, so the upload does not deduplicate: uploading the same file again adds those rows again.
